The one idea behind it

Somebody is always forced to hedge. That flow is measurable.

When you buy an option, a market maker — a dealer — is usually on the other side. Dealers do not want a directional bet. They want the fee. So they hedge, and the hedge is where the story starts.

  1. 01

    You buy the option

    A dealer takes the other side, whether or not they like the direction.

  2. 02

    The dealer hedges flat

    They buy or sell the stock until the position carries no view. Just the fee.

  3. 03

    Price moves. The hedge is wrong again.

    The right hedge changes with every tick, so staying flat means trading again — mechanically, in a direction the math dictates.

  4. 04

    That trading is visible

    It is large, it is predictable, and it is sitting in the options chain for anyone who measures it. Measuring it is the whole app.

If you learn one thing here, make it dealer gamma. The glossary explains it without the jargon, and the Gamma screen shows it live.
