The one idea behind it

Somebody is always forced to hedge. That flow is measurable.

When you buy an option, a professional market maker — a dealer — usually takes the other side. Dealers do not want a directional bet. They want the fee. So they hedge.

  1. 01

    You buy the option

    A dealer takes the other side of your trade, whether they like the direction or not.

  2. 02

    They hedge to stay neutral

    They buy or sell the underlying stock so the position carries no view — only the fee.

  3. 03

    Price moves, the hedge breaks

    The required hedge changes with price. Staying neutral means trading again — mechanically.

  4. 04

    That flow is visible

    Large, predictable and sitting in the options chain — if you measure it. That is the whole app.

If you only ever learn one concept here, make it dealer gamma — explained in plain language under Dealer Positioning.
