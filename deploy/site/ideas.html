Trade ideas

One trade an hour, from the live scanner.

Every hour of the regular session, from 8:35 to 2:35 Central, NeuralStrike picks one graded trade from the live Market Scanner and posts it here, on X, on Discord and on Telegram. Each card shows the legs, the grade, the most it can lose, the most it can make and the probability of profit. Pick a day to see what was proposed; open a card to see it full size.

Each idea is followed under the app's own exit rules: it closes the first time it reaches its profit target or its stop, and otherwise settles at expiry. The option's value is modelled from the stock's price, minute by minute, at the volatility its entry price implied, so open figures are estimates.
